Engineering Abiotic Stress Tolerance Traits for Mitigating Climate Change

Sumit Jangra, Aakash Mishra, Disha Kamboj, Neelam R. Yadav, Ram C. Yadav

Springer, book chapter · 2017

Google Scholar ↗Cite

A review of engineering abiotic stress tolerance in crops to offset the effects of climate change.

Overview

Book chapter in Plant Biotechnology: Recent Advancements and Developments (Springer, 2017), pages 59 to 73. It reviews approaches to engineering abiotic stress tolerance traits in plants as a way to mitigate the effects of climate change on crops.
