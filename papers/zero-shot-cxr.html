Improving Zero-Shot Detection of Low Prevalence Chest Pathologies using Domain Pre-trained Language Models

Aakash Mishra, Rajat Mittal, Christy Jestin, Kostas Tingos, Pranav Rajpurkar

MIDL 2023, short paper · Apr 2023

arXivCodeCite

Swapping a CLIP model's text tower for one pre-trained on clinical text hurts on common chest findings but helps a lot on rare ones.

Abstract

Recent advances in zero-shot learning have enabled the use of paired image-text data to replace structured labels, removing the need for expert-annotated datasets. Models such as CLIP-based CheXzero use these advances for chest X-ray interpretation.

We hypothesize that domain pre-trained models such as CXR-BERT, BlueBERT, and ClinicalBERT can improve CLIP-like models with domain knowledge by replacing BERT weights, at the cost of breaking the original model's alignment. We evaluate zero-shot classification models with domain-specific pre-training for detecting low-prevalence pathologies. Even though replacing the weights of the original CLIP-BERT degrades performance on common pathologies, pre-trained text towers perform much better on low-prevalence diseases. This motivates future ensembles that combine differently trained language models.
