Going Beyond ImageNet Preprocessing for X-ray Vision Models

Building a CR image preprocessor from the ground up

Arjun Karpur, Aakash Mishra

Epsilon Health Research · Aug 19, 2026

Read the post ↗Cite

X-rays are not natural images, so their preprocessing shouldn't be borrowed from ImageNet. We measure what makes CR images different, build a pipeline for them, and ablate each design choice.

Abstract

Most vision models for radiology are fed by a preprocessing pipeline inherited from natural images: normalize the pixels, crop or squeeze the frame into a fixed square, and hand it to the backbone. X-ray images are not natural images, however, and the preprocessing pipeline should reflect this. In this post, we identify and measure a handful of image characteristics specific to CR (computed radiography) and propose our CR image preprocessing pipeline. We run a series of ablation experiments to study the sensitivity of pathology classification models to the image preprocessor's design decisions.
