A review of how plants respond to drought and the approaches used to make crops more drought-tolerant.

Overview

Book chapter in Approaches for Enhancing Abiotic Stress Tolerance in Plants (CRC Press, 2019), pages 79 to 98. It reviews plant responses to drought stress and the approaches used to improve drought tolerance.
